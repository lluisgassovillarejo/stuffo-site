Website privacy and feedback

Updated 20 October 2026. This notice covers this website. The app privacy policy remains separate.

Optional website analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you choose Allow analytics. It measures page visits, content actions and clicks to Google Play. We send an app identifier, content identifier, page type and button placement. Page URLs sent to analytics omit query strings and fragments; referring URLs are reduced to their origin. We do not send app contents, feedback text, email addresses, budget entries or precise locations to analytics.

Your choice is stored in this browser. You can withdraw it using Website analytics choices at the bottom of any page. Declining does not block the guides, resources, Google Play links or feedback.

Feedback

The feedback link opens Google Forms. If you submit, the answers you choose to provide are stored in a private Google Forms response sheet and reviewed by the app developer. Contact details are optional and are used for a reply only if you request contact. Do not include private financial records, passwords, routes or precise locations. Responses are not published as a public results summary. Google and the hosting provider also process requests according to their own privacy notices.

For questions or a request concerning feedback you submitted, contact the developer using the contact details in the app privacy policy. Please identify the app and approximate submission date.
