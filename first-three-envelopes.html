The Stuffo field guide

Your first three budget envelopes

A small budget you can keep up with is a useful starting point. Choose three categories that reflect your own expenses.

Choose categories you recognize

Groceries, transport and fun are examples, not a prescription. Use names that make it obvious where a purchase belongs. You can add other categories as your routine develops.

Assign money you have

Write the amount you are planning with, then choose your allocations. An amount in an envelope is part of your plan; it is not a transfer between bank accounts.

Fictional worksheet example — not a suggested budget
ItemAmount

$100 + $40 + $30 + $330 = $500. This example is not a complete household budget.

Leave room to decide later

You do not have to allocate everything immediately. Keep unallocated money distinct from what remains inside an envelope so you do not count the same money twice.
