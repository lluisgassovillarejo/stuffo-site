The Stuffo field guide

About Stuffo: manual envelope budgeting for Android

Stuffo is an Android app by Lluís Gassó for people who want to plan with envelopes and record spending themselves.

What does it do?

Create envelopes, allocate income, record purchases, move allocations between envelopes, and track savings goals with target amounts and dates. Balances reflect the entries you make. The monthly summary brings the recorded totals together.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. Stuffo does not link bank accounts, import purchases or move money. You make payments as usual and enter them in your budget record.

Does it need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed. Core budgeting works offline. Installation and store access require a connection.

Is there an iPhone version?

The current public release is on Android. This page does not announce an iPhone version or a release date.

Which languages and currencies does it support?

The current release is in English with one USD budget. It does not convert currencies.

Does it sync across devices?

There is no automatic cross-device budget sync. You can save and restore a backup file. Backup files contain readable budget details; keep them somewhere appropriate. A storage provider you choose may sync the file, but Stuffo does not upload it for you.
